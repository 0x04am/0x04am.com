## Who am I?

I'm a developer who does my best thinking after midnight. I build AI agents, tinker with systems software, and occasionally ship things into production at 4am when nobody's watching.

This blog is where I write about what I'm building, what I'm learning, and what broke at 3:59am.

## What I work on

- **AI & LLMs** — agents, RAG pipelines, fine-tuning, evals
- **Systems** — Rust, Go, low-level networking, distributed systems
- **Infrastructure** — containers, edge compute, the whole boring stack that makes things actually work

## Why "0x04am"?

`0x04` is `4` in hex. `4am` is when the best ideas happen and the worst decisions get made. It's also when Stack Overflow is quiet and you have to actually think.
